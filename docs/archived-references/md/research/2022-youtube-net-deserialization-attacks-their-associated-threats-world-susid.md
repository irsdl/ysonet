## Description

We will briefly cover the history of deserialization vulnerabilities and explain what they are, as well as describe how they can potentially expose websites using Umbraco Content Management System (CMS) to high-severity attacks. We will highlight typical scenarios in the context of a CMS and demonstrate some applicable techniques using concrete examples of .Net deserialization exploitation. We will then also look at some ways to discover and prevent .Net deserialization vulnerabilities. Finally, we will perform a hands-on exercise discovering, exploiting, and preventing a deserialization vulnerability in a CMS plugin.
